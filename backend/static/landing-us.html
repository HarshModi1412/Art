The opportunity

The money is already in your shop. It leaves quietly.

Not because you are doing anything wrong, but because one person cannot watch everything at once. A brand with eight people watches all of it. Here is what goes out the door while nobody is looking, and what closing it is worth.

The customers who just stop buying

They do not complain. They do not unsubscribe. They simply do not come back, and you find out months later. Getting one of them back costs an email; finding a new one costs an ad.

If 40 past buyers who spent $60 each are sitting uncontacted, that is $2,400 of goodwill you already paid for.

The bestseller that was out of stock

Every day your best item is sold out is a sale that goes to somebody else, and a customer who learns you run out.

One popular item, out for six days a month, at $35 a sale and two sales a day: $420 a month, gone without a trace.

The week you did not post

The brands growing on Instagram are not more talented. They are more regular. Four posts a week, every week, is the whole trick, and it is the first thing to go when you are packing orders.

Posting is the cheapest customer you will ever get. Skipping it costs nothing today and everything in a year.

None of this is a knowledge problem. Every big brand knows these three things. They have people to act on them. That is the only difference, and it is the difference this closes, for one person, in the time it takes to drink a coffee.

Built for Shopify's customer. Built to Odoo's standard.

Two kinds of software already serve small sellers, and each gets half of it right.

Shopify

Right customer. A person who sells things, set up in an afternoon, no consultant.

Thin where it counts. The storefront is the product; the operations are apps. Inventory is an app. Win-back email is an app. Reports are an app. Each is billed separately every month, and none of them talk to each other.

Odoo

Right depth. Everything is joined up. Buying, stock and sales are one system, so a delivery moves your stock without you typing it twice.

Wrong customer. Built for a business with someone employed to set it up. A jewelry seller with 40 designs will not get through the setup, and the screens assume you were trained on them.

One Tap Manager

Joined-up insides. Products, stock, suppliers, orders and customers are one thing, not six. A sale moves your stock, shows up in your numbers and in your customer list, without you entering anything twice.

Shopify's front door. Sign up, connect a channel or upload a CSV, and the first screen tells you what to do this morning rather than asking you to set up a system first.

The difference in one sentence: Shopify tells you what happened and sells you an app to work out what to do. We tell you what to do, and the doing is already wired to the same data. The Shopify comparison · The Odoo comparison
What we are not, yet. We do not do payroll, full accounting, sales tax filing or several warehouses. If you need those, keep your accounting software; One Tap Manager works next to it. If you sell 40 to 400 products and want to know what to do next, that gap costs you nothing.

Questions

Questions sellers ask before they start

What is One Tap Manager?

One Tap Manager is shop management software for small online sellers. It reads the sales you already have, from Shopify, Amazon, Instagram or a spreadsheet, and every morning it tells you the few things worth doing: what to reorder, which customers to win back, and what to post. Then it does most of that work for you, from the purchase order to the Instagram captions.

Is One Tap Manager the same as other One Tap apps?

No. One Tap Manager is not related to the other apps called One Tap or OneTap, or to Google One Tap sign-in. It is the shop manager for small online sellers, at onetapmanager.com (one word in the address, three words in the name).

How much does One Tap Manager cost?

Every account starts with a 7-day free trial of Pro Max, with no card. After that, Pro is $10 a month and includes everything except AI image generation. Pro Max is $12.99 a month and adds AI product photos and short product clips. There is no setup fee and we never take a percentage of your sales.

Is there a free plan?

There is a 7-day free trial with every feature, and no card is needed to start it. There is no free plan after the trial: to keep using the app, you choose Pro or Pro Max. Your data is kept either way.

What is the difference between Pro and Pro Max?

Pro runs the whole shop: sales analytics, customer groups, win-back emails, stock and reorder alerts, purchase orders, the Instagram planner and review analysis, with unlimited AI writing. Pro Max adds AI image generation: product photos made from your own product photo, a picture for every planned post, and short product clips.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. You sign up with an email and a password, or with Google, and the 7-day trial starts right away. You only add a card when you choose a plan.

Does it work with Shopify and Amazon?

Yes. You can connect a Shopify store with an access token from a custom app in your Shopify admin, or Amazon Seller Central with Selling Partner API keys, and the orders flow into the same analytics. You can also upload a CSV or Excel export from any POS or spreadsheet.

How long does it take to set up?

About two minutes. Connect your store, or export a month of sales as a CSV file and drop it in. One Tap Manager works out which column is the date, the amount, the customer and the product, you confirm any it had to guess, and the first insights are on screen.

Who is One Tap Manager for?

Small online sellers of clothing, jewelry, fragrance and similar products, selling on Shopify, Amazon, Instagram or their own site. It is built for one owner running the whole business, not a team of analysts.

Can it run my Instagram for me?

Yes. It plans a whole week as one story rather than seven unrelated posts, writes the captions and picks the times. You approve, and it schedules and publishes them. On Pro Max it also makes the pictures, starting from your own product photos.

Will it tell me when to reorder stock?

Yes, on both Pro and Pro Max. It learns how fast each item sells and how long each supplier takes, warns you before you sell out, and hands you the purchase order for that supplier already filled in.

Is my data safe and private?

Your sales data is yours. Third-party credentials and your uploaded data are encrypted, and the companies that store data for us are named in the privacy policy rather than hidden behind vague wording. You can ask for your data back or deleted at any time.

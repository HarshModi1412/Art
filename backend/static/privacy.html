Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

1. Who we are

Content Seller ("we", "us", "our") is a business tool that turns a seller's sales, product and customer data into plain-language recommendations, and — where a seller chooses to connect their Instagram Business account — helps them create, schedule and publish content. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is protected, including data accessed through Meta Platforms, Inc. ("Meta") via the Instagram Graph API.

2. What data we collect

Account data. Email address and a hashed password when you create a Content Seller account.

Business data you upload. Sales, inventory, order and customer files (e.g. CSV/Excel) that you choose to upload, used solely to generate the analytics and recommendations you request.

Instagram / Meta data (only if you connect Instagram). When you connect your Instagram Business account via Meta's OAuth login, we receive a long-lived access token and basic account identifiers (your Instagram Business Account ID and username) needed to publish content on your behalf. We do not receive your Instagram password — authentication happens entirely on Meta's login page.

Content you create in the app. Captions, hashtags and images generated or edited through our Content Creator, and a record of what was posted or scheduled and when.

Usage data. Basic technical logs (timestamps, request paths, error traces) used to keep the service running and to debug issues.

3. How we use Instagram / Meta data

If you connect an Instagram Business account, we use the instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and business_management permissions strictly to:

  • Identify which Instagram Business account you are publishing to;
  • Create and publish the image/caption posts you approve, either immediately or on a schedule you set;
  • Show you basic status information about your connected account inside Content Seller.

We do not use this access to read your private messages, post without your approval, or access data unrelated to the content you create in the app. We do not sell Instagram/Meta data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

4. How we store and protect data

  • Access tokens are stored encrypted at rest and are only decrypted server-side at the moment a post is published.
  • Uploaded business files and generated content are stored on our hosting provider's infrastructure and are only accessible to your authenticated account.
  • We use industry-standard transport security (HTTPS/TLS) for all traffic between your browser, our servers, and Meta's API.

5. Data retention & deletion

We retain your account data, uploaded files and connected-account tokens for as long as your account is active. You can:

  • Disconnect Instagram at any time from the Instagram module inside Content Seller — this immediately revokes and deletes the stored access token.
  • Request full account deletion by emailing us at privacy@contentseller.app. We will delete your account data, uploaded files, generated content and any stored tokens within 30 days, except where we are required to retain records by law.

6. Third-party sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data with the following categories of third party only as needed to operate the service:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — to publish content to Instagram on your behalf, via the Instagram Graph API, when you connect your account.
  • OpenAI — to generate suggested captions, hashtags and images when you use AI-assisted content generation. Only the prompt you trigger (e.g. product type, tone) is sent; we do not send your customer or sales data to this provider.
  • Our hosting provider — to store and serve the application and your data securely.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting privacy@contentseller.app. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable data protection law (e.g. India's DPDP Act, EU/UK GDPR); we will honour verifiable requests consistent with those laws.

8. Children's privacy

Content Seller is a business tool intended for sellers and merchants. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@contentseller.app

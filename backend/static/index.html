AI Business Analyst

The shape of an answer, not a real one

These three cards are illustrations of the FORM the analyst uses. Every figure in them is invented. Yours are worked out from the file you upload, and each one shows the arithmetic behind it.

Which day quietly loses money

It compares each weekday against your own average, names the one that is behind, and suggests the smallest change that would move it.

Which product earns less than it looks like

Volume and margin are read separately, so a bestseller that is thin on margin is named as one rather than praised as a top seller.

This is what the AI Analyst gives you: specific actions with the arithmetic shown, worked out from your own data.

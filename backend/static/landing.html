Chapter 2 · What it is costing you

The money is already in your shop. It leaves quietly.

Not because you are doing anything wrong, but because one person cannot watch everything at once. Here is what walks out of a small shop every month.

The customers who just stop coming

They do not complain. They simply do not come back, and you find out months later. Getting one back costs a message. Finding a new one costs an ad.

If 40 past buyers who spent ₹3,000 each are sitting uncontacted, that is ₹1.2 lakh of goodwill you already paid for.

The bestseller that was out of stock

Every day your best item is not on the shelf is a sale that goes to somebody else, and a customer who learns you run out.

One popular item, out for six days a month, at ₹900 a sale and two sales a day: ₹10,800 a month, gone without a trace.

The “price?” that went cold

Every DM that waits hours for a price is a buyer who found it somewhere else. A website link answers “price?”, “size?” and “available?” in one tap, at 2 am too.

If 4 buyers a week give up waiting, at ₹900 an order: 4 × 4 weeks × ₹900 = ₹14,400 a month you never see.

The week you did not post

The brands growing on Instagram are not more talented. They are more regular. Four posts a week is the whole trick, and it is the first thing to go when you are busy.

Posting is the cheapest customer you will ever get. Skipping it costs nothing today and everything in a year.

Add it up: ₹10,800 + ₹14,400 = ₹25,200 a month walking out of the door, plus ₹1.2 lakh of old customers you already paid to win. Your team costs ₹1,299.

Why old customers matter most: repeat buyers are about 21% of a typical online shop's customers, but they bring in about 44% of its revenue (source: Gorgias, repeat customer rate benchmarks).

The ₹ figures above are worked examples with the arithmetic shown, not results from a customer. We are new and would rather you check our maths than trust our marketing.

Questions

Questions sellers ask before they start

What is One Tap Manager?

One Tap Manager is an app that runs a small Indian online shop for you, end to end, like a team. It brings back customers who stopped buying with a WhatsApp message written for each one, warns you before your bestsellers run out and fills in the order, gives you your own selling website with 0% commission, plans your Instagram, makes product photos and writes a GST bill for every website order. Every morning it shows you the three jobs that make you money today, already done, and you tap to approve.

Is One Tap Manager the same as other One Tap apps?

One Tap Manager is not related to the other apps called One Tap or OneTap, or to Google One Tap sign-in. It is the shop manager for small Indian sellers, at onetapmanager.com (one word in the address, three words in the name).

Do I need a website already?

No, it works either way. Add 3 products, with a photo, a name and a price, choose a look, add your UPI ID, and your website is live at onetapmanager.com/yourshop, or on your own domain. Share the link on WhatsApp and in your Instagram bio, and buyers order there instead of asking the price in your DMs. If you already sell on Shopify, Amazon, WooCommerce or Wix, connect it or upload a sales file instead.

How do my customers pay on my website?

By UPI straight to your own UPI ID, by cash on delivery, with an optional small advance to confirm the order, or by card, netbanking and wallets through your own Razorpay account. The money goes to you, never through us.

Do you take a commission on my sales?

No. One Tap Manager is a flat monthly price. There is no commission, no per-order fee and no percentage of your sales, however much you sell.

How much does One Tap Manager cost?

Every account starts with 14 days free, with the whole team and no card. After that, Pro Max is ₹1,299 a month and includes every job, including AI product photos and short product clips. If you do not need photos, Pro is ₹700 a month. We never take a percentage of your sales.

Will I be auto-debited after the trial?

No. Nothing renews on its own. When the trial ends, the app asks you to choose a plan, and each paid month runs for 30 days. After that, you choose to pay again from the Account tab. There is no mandate and no surprise deduction.

What if I pay and it is not for me?

Ask for a refund within 7 days of the payment and you get all of it back. No reason needed. The refund goes back the way you paid, and the full policy is on our refunds page.

Is there a free plan?

There is a 14-day free trial with every feature, and no card is needed to start it. There is no free plan after the trial: to keep using the app, you choose Pro Max or Pro. Your data is kept either way.

Do I need a card or bank details to start?

No. You sign up with just an email and a password and the 14-day trial starts right away. You only add a payment method when you choose a plan.

How long does it take to set up?

Your website takes about 15 minutes: 3 products, a look and your UPI ID. If you already have orders, export them as a CSV or Excel file from your billing app and drop it in. One Tap Manager works out which column is the date, the amount, the customer and the product, you confirm any it had to guess, and the first answers are on screen in about two minutes.

Do I need any technical or accounting skill to use it?

No. Everything is written in plain sentences rather than charts you have to decode, and each job comes with the work already done. If you can read a WhatsApp message, you can use it.

Who is One Tap Manager for?

Anyone in India who sells clothes, jewellery, perfume or similar products online, with a website or without one: on Instagram, WhatsApp, Amazon, Shopify or their own site. It is built for one owner running the whole business, not a team of analysts.

How is it different from my billing or accounting app?

A billing app records what already happened. One Tap Manager reads those records and tells you what to do next, then does most of it for you: the reorder, the win-back message, the week of posts. It works alongside your billing app rather than replacing it.

Can it run my Instagram for me?

It plans a whole week as one story rather than seven unrelated posts, writes the captions, picks the times and includes posts for festivals like Diwali and Navratri. On Pro Max it also makes the pictures from your own product photos. You approve, and it schedules them.

Will it tell me when to reorder stock?

Yes, on both Pro Max and Pro. It learns how fast each item sells and how long each supplier takes, warns you before you run out, and hands you the purchase order for that supplier already filled in.

Is my data safe and private?

Your sales data is yours. Third-party credentials and your uploaded data are encrypted, and the exact companies that store data for us, Render, Supabase and Razorpay, are named in the privacy policy rather than hidden behind vague wording. You can ask for your data back or deleted at any time.

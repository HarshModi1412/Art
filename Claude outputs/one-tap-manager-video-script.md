# One Tap Manager: 45-second Motion Graphics Script

Source: public site onetapmanager.com (the /smart workspace is behind a login, so its tabs were not opened; everything below comes from the landing page's own descriptions).

## What it can do (feature list)

1. **Sales analytics**: upload one CSV/Excel sales file, get answers in about 2 minutes. Category and sub-category trends, weak days (e.g. "Tuesdays run 41% below average"), profit vs. volume, next-month view. Plain-sentence insights.
2. **Customer intelligence**: best customers, frequent buyers, and who has gone quiet.
3. **Win-back campaigns**: WhatsApp message, coupon or Excel list written for you; it counts how many customers actually came back. Unlimited on Free.
4. **Stock and supplier ordering (Max)**: knows sell rate and supplier lead time, tells you when to reorder, fills in the purchase order, sends a PDF PO to the supplier.
5. **Reviews and complaint analysis**: finds the loudest complaint (e.g. "sizing runs small") and gives a fix-first plan. Market position and reputation reports.
6. **AI photo studio**: learn your brand look from 5 of your photos, then generate product pictures in that style starting from your real product photo.
7. **Instagram planner**: a week planned as one story (tease, reveal, proof, close), captions written, times chosen, pictures made, you approve and it schedules.
8. **Invoices and GST**: proper tax invoices with correct rates, plus one file for the accountant.
9. **Free selling website**: included on every tier, with orders. An order updates stock, uses materials, writes the invoice and appears in your numbers automatically.
10. **Morning digest**: the three things worth doing today, by email and WhatsApp.
11. **Multilingual output**: English, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi.
12. **Pricing**: Free forever (up to 250 products); Max Rs 999/month; credit packs that never expire. No cut of your sales.

## Look and feel (suggested)

- 16:9 and 9:16 cut for Reels. Dark UI with one warm accent (the "1T" mark). Big Indian-rupee numerals as the hero graphic.
- Style: kinetic typography plus simplified UI cards sliding in. Whoosh/tick SFX on every card, upbeat lo-fi/electro-Indian beat around 110 BPM.
- Voiceover: warm, fast, conversational. A Hindi or Hinglish version can swap in the Hindi line under each scene.

Keep scenes 1, 3, 4, 5, 7. Compress scene 3 to two cards and drop scene 2 and 6. Trim voiceover to: "Running a shop alone? One Tap Manager reads your sales, shows who stopped coming and what's about to run out, writes the message, fills the order, and plans your Instagram week. Start free."

## Notes before you produce it

- The site labels all its figures ("38 customers", "-41%", "3 days left") as worked examples, not customer results. Keep an "Example data" tag in the corner of scenes 3 and 4 so the video stays honest, and do not add testimonials.
- Rs 7,600 is the site's own typical-app-stack estimate for Shopify sellers. If you show it, label it "typical app costs".
- Sections marked (Max) in the feature list are paid after launch; the site says everything is free during launch.
- The in-app tabs (dashboard, analytics, customers, stock, content) were not viewed. If you share a login-free demo or screen recordings, I can match the UI in each scene to the real screens.

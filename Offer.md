## 2. The dream outcome

> **"A team that brings your shop more money, while you get your evenings back."**

It is not "a team". It is **a team that earns you money**:

- **More money:** old customers come back, bestsellers stay in stock, "price?" turns into orders on your website. Repeat buyers are about 21% of a typical online shop's customers and about 44% of its revenue (Gorgias, repeat customer rate benchmarks).
- **Status:** your own website and brand-quality photos, like the big brands.
- **Freedom:** a few taps in the morning instead of 14-hour days. Dinner with your family.
- **A customer base that grows by itself:** every website order captures the customer.

## 3. The value equation, lever by lever

**Value = (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood) ÷ (Time delay × Effort and sacrifice)**, with risk reversal on top.

| Lever | What the page does |
|---|---|
| **Dream outcome, up** | Hero: "Get a 7-person team that brings your shop more money." Chapter 2 shows the money leaking now with the arithmetic (₹25,200 a month plus ₹1.2 lakh of forgotten customers), against a ₹1,299 team |
| **Perceived likelihood, up** | Hormozi's Proof Checklist: *"Your promise is not a differentiator. Your proof is."* With no customers yet, the proof is getting a **result before paying**: website live and first messages sent inside the 14 free days. Also a practice shop (90 days, 726 orders), "it runs on your own sales", "it counts who came back", a 4-language note and the Journal of Marketing research behind the planner |
| **Time delay, down** | Three steps: start free (30 seconds), show it your shop (about 15 minutes), tap every morning |
| **Effort and sacrifice, down** | "Done for you, end to end. Your only job is one tap." Every team member card ends with the seller's part in 1 to 3 words ("You: tap send"). A before and after table. They keep selling the way they do now |
| **Risk, down** | 6 promises, all already true: ₹0 for 14 days with no card; results before you pay; no auto-debit (`billing.py`: nothing renews on its own); full refund within 7 days of any payment (`legal.py`); 0% commission; your data stays yours. Together: **21 days with nothing to lose** |

## 6. Bold claims, insecurities, and the line we kept

You asked for grey-area claims and dark insecurities. On the page:

- **Bold claims:**
  - "7-person team"
  - "brings your shop more money"
  - "you just tap"
  - "takes orders while you sleep"
  - "about 15 minutes"
  - typical tool prices
  - people costing "more than ₹1,299 a week"
- **Insecurities:**
  - the shop stops when you stop
  - customers forgetting you
  - a worse competitor growing faster
  - the business living in one phone
  - family eating without you
  - "next month, exactly like this month"

**What we did not do, on purpose:**

- **No invented proof:** no invented customers, counts, ratings or testimonials, and no revenue % promise.
- **No shaming the "no" button:** the "two paths" section states the stakes, but there is no "No thanks, I like losing money" link. That is **confirm shaming**, one of the 13 dark patterns India's CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines (2023) name.
- **Nothing your tests block:** they reject fake counters, fake quotes and unlabelled figures.

The footer also says the Chapter 1 day is a typical day, not a real seller's.

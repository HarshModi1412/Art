# How to use this file

1. Go post by post. Every grey box is one prompt. Copy it, paste it, generate.
2. **Image prompts** go into ChatGPT, Gemini or Ideogram. **Video prompts** go into Kling, Veo, Runway or Hailuo, using "image to video", 9:16, 10 seconds.
3. Every video shot has two prompts. Make the **First frame** image first, pick the most real-looking one, upload it to your video tool, then paste the **Motion** prompt.
4. Make 3 takes of everything. Keep the one where the hands have five fingers and nothing melts.
5. If the AI misspells text or changes an app screenshot, generate it again without the text and type the text yourself in Canva or CapCut. App screens are always your real screenshots and recordings.
6. Build each reel in CapCut from its **Timeline**. It tells you which seconds of which clip to use, what text goes on screen, and which sound to add.
7. **Attach** means: upload that file together with the prompt.

**4. The phone frame for app recordings** (image prompt)

```

```

In CapCut: put the screen recording on the bottom layer, the phone frame on top, and use **Chroma key** on the green so the recording shows through.

**5. CapCut styles** (set once, reuse in every reel)

- **Hook text:** Inter ExtraBold (or the closest heavy sans in CapCut), off-white #F7F5EF on a navy #111827 box with rounded corners. Maximum 7 words per line, 2 lines on screen. Keep it in the middle of the screen, away from the top 12% and the bottom 22%, where Instagram's buttons sit.
- **Navy card:** a solid #111827 background with off-white text. The key words in marigold #F5A623.
- **Numbers:** a bold monospace font (JetBrains Mono or similar). Money you keep is in mint green #34D399.
- **Chat bubbles:** light beige #EFECE3 rounded boxes, a thin dark border, dark text. Never copy WhatsApp's or Instagram's design.
- **Tags:** tiny grey monospace capitals in the bottom-left corner. "AI GENERATED" on every AI shot. "SAMPLE SHOP DATA" on every app recording.
- **Zooms on app screens:** a fast "snap zoom" (100% to 130% in about 0.15 s) onto the exact number or button named in the timeline, held for at least 0.8 s.

**6. Sound kit.** Phone buzz, bass hit, cash "ting", soft "bonk", record scratch, heartbeat, whoosh, pop, click, ding. The **sonic logo** closes every reel: a soft tap on glass, then a two-note rising chime. **Music** comes from Instagram's own audio library on the day you post, kept low under the sound effects.

# Growth rules for these 12 posts

1. **Set up the auto-DM before Post 1.** Every "Comment WORD" must get its DM within minutes. Use a Meta-approved tool (for example ManyChat's Instagram automation) and test each keyword from a second account. An unanswered keyword costs you trust.
2. **The first hour after posting decides the reach.** Reply to every comment in that hour, and send the post in DMs to 20 real sellers you would like as customers. Early saves, shares and comments from the right people tell Instagram who to show it to next.
3. **Post the pinned comment** (given under each post) the moment the post goes live, and pin it.
4. **Share every post to your Story** the same day, with a poll or question sticker.
5. **Collab posts are your free reach.** When a seller from the 0 to 20 challenge goes live, make a short reel of her store and publish it as an Instagram Collab with her account. It appears on both profiles and in front of her followers, who are exactly your customers.
6. **Same time, every post day.** Pick one time and keep it for all 3 weeks so your followers learn when you show up. Start with the evening, after shops close, and move it if Insights shows your audience is online at another hour.
7. **Every Friday, look at three numbers per reel:** 3-second hold (did the hook stop the thumb), shares and saves. Make more of whatever gets shared.

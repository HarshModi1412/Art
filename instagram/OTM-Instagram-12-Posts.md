## How to use this file

For each post:
1. Open a new chat in your image or video AI.
2. Paste the **instruction block** from Brand Language section 0.
3. Attach **OTM-Instagram-Brand-Language.md** and the **reference images** listed under "Attach".
4. Paste **one prompt at a time** (each prompt is self-contained).
5. Put all words on the design yourself in Canva or CapCut, exactly as written in the "On screen" column, using the template named (T1 to T6, defined below).
6. Run the pre-publish checklist (Brand Language section 12).

Prompt codes: `P4-C2` means Post 4, clip 2. `P1-S1` means Post 1, slide 1. `A0` is a master reference asset you make once.

## Part A. What changed from your original plan, and why

| Your plan | Refined | Why |
|---|---|---|

It does not import from an Instagram handle. Showing a feature that does not exist breaks Brand.md rule 1 |

| "6 languages" | "Hindi, Tamil and Kannada" | That is what the app interface ships in today |
| Supplier orders shown as free | "Free during launch" chip | It is a Max feature once launch mode ends |
| Hooks were plain statements | Each post now uses one of the 10 viral hook patterns, matched to what the post can pay off | A hook only works when the reveal is worth it (your hook file's own rule) |
| No reason to follow | Three series, a promise on every end card, and four free lead magnets (Appendix) | People follow a page for what they'll get next week, not for an app |
| Post 12 was a soft CTA at the end | It becomes the "0 to 20" public challenge and moves to week 1 | A founder with zero customers saying so, with a deadline, is the strongest follow reason you have. It is also honest |

**Real pain:** Customers ask "website hai?". Their "link in bio" is just a WhatsApp number. A cousin or agency quotes thousands of rupees and "next week pakka". Marketplaces take a cut.

### STOCK (Post 8): when to reorder, cheat sheet
```
1. For each bestseller, write: how many you sell per day (last 30 days' sales ÷ 30).
2. Write: how many days your supplier takes to deliver.
3. Add spare days: 3 to 5 in normal months, 7 or more before festivals.
4. Buy-again level = per-day sales × (supplier days + spare days).
5. When stock left falls to that number, order today.
Example (illustrative): 2 a day × (5 supplier days + 3 spare) = 16. When you're down to 16 pieces, order.
6. How much to order: per-day sales × days until your next order, plus the spare.
One Tap Manager does this for every product automatically and fills in the purchase order: onetapmanager.com
```

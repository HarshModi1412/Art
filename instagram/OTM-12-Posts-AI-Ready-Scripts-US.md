# How to use this file

1. Go post by post. Every gray box is one prompt. Copy it, paste it, generate.
2. **Image prompts** go into ChatGPT, Gemini or Ideogram. **Video prompts** go into Kling, Veo, Runway or Hailuo, using "image to video", 9:16, 10 seconds.
3. Every video shot has two prompts. Make the **First frame** image first, pick the most real-looking one, upload it to your video tool, then paste the **Motion** prompt.
4. Make 3 takes of everything. Keep the one where the hands have five fingers and nothing melts.
5. If the AI misspells text or changes an app screenshot, generate it again without the text and type the text yourself in Canva or CapCut. App screens are always your real screenshots and recordings.
6. Build each reel in CapCut from its **Timeline**. It tells you which seconds of which clip to use, what text goes on screen, and which sound to add.
7. **Attach** means: upload that file together with the prompt.

# Before you post these to US sellers

The scripts are ready. Five things in the app are not ready for a US seller yet, and a US viewer who taps through will hit them:

1. **Card checkout is not switched on.** Sellers can save Stripe or PayPal keys, but the code still marks card charging as not ready. Posts 2 and 12 send sellers to open a store, so hold them until a real card payment goes through end to end.
2. **The store setup still speaks India.** It ends by asking for a UPI ID, and the checkout asks for a "PIN code" and drops any letters. A US seller needs a card step, and US shoppers need a "ZIP code" field.
3. **The new pricing is not in the app yet.** The pricing page still shows ₹0 forever and ₹999. Put in the 7-day free trial, Pro at $10 and Pro Max at $12.99 before you record Post 9.
4. **The sample shop is Indian.** Your screenshots and recordings show kurtas, sarees, rupees and Indian names. Make a US version of the sample shop (dollar prices, US product and customer names) and re-record every app screen. Then change any number in these scripts (like 10.6% or 3.2 days) to match what the new recording shows.
5. **The Christmas campaign in the holiday planner was written for India** (it uses hashtags like #31stNight). Rewrite it for the US before you record Post 6.

**5. CapCut styles** (set once, reuse in every reel)

- **Hook text:** Inter ExtraBold (or the closest heavy sans in CapCut), off-white #F7F5EF on a navy #111827 box with rounded corners. Maximum 7 words per line, 2 lines on screen. Keep it in the middle of the screen, away from the top 12% and the bottom 22%, where Instagram's buttons sit.
- **Navy card:** a solid #111827 background with off-white text. The key words in marigold #F5A623.
- **Numbers:** a bold monospace font (JetBrains Mono or similar). Money the seller keeps is in mint green #34D399.
- **Chat bubbles:** light beige #EFECE3 rounded boxes, a thin dark border, dark text. Never copy Instagram's or any messaging app's design.
- **Tags:** tiny gray monospace capitals in the bottom-left corner. "AI GENERATED" on every AI shot. "SAMPLE SHOP DATA" on every app recording.
- **Zooms on app screens:** a fast "snap zoom" (100% to 130% in about 0.15 s) onto the exact number or button named in the timeline, held for at least 0.8 s.

**6. Sound kit.** Phone buzz, bass hit, cash register "ding", soft "bonk", record scratch, heartbeat, whoosh, pop, click, ding. The **sonic logo** closes every reel: a soft tap on glass, then a two-note rising chime. **Music** comes from Instagram's own audio library on the day you post, kept low under the sound effects.

# Growth rules for these 12 posts

1. **Set up the auto-DM before Post 1.** Every "Comment WORD" must get its DM within minutes. Use a Meta-approved tool (for example ManyChat's Instagram automation) and test each keyword from a second account. An unanswered keyword costs you trust.
2. **The first hour after posting decides the reach.** Reply to every comment in that hour, and send the post in DMs to 20 real US sellers you would like as customers. Early saves, shares and comments from the right people tell Instagram who to show it to next.
3. **Post the pinned comment** (given under each post) the moment the post goes live, and pin it.
4. **Share every post to your Story** the same day, with a poll or question sticker.
5. **Collab posts are your free reach.** When a seller from the 0 to 20 challenge goes live, make a short reel of her store and publish it as an Instagram Collab with her account. It appears on both profiles and in front of her followers, who are exactly your customers.
6. **Post on US time.** Start with 7 pm Eastern, when shop owners are done for the day. That is about 4:30 to 5:30 am in India, so schedule every post in Meta Business Suite and let it go live while you sleep. Move the time if Insights shows your followers are online at another hour.
7. **Every Friday, look at three numbers per reel:** 3-second hold (did the hook stop the thumb), shares and saves. Make more of whatever gets shared.

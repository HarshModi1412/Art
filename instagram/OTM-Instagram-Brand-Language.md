## 1. What this page is, in one breath

**One Tap Manager is the back-office team a one-person Indian shop never had, and our Instagram is where that team shows up every week to fix one real shop problem for free, with a laugh.**

The page has three jobs, in this order:

1. **Be useful for free.** Every post leaves a seller with something they can use today even if they never sign up.
2. **Be the funniest honest voice in Indian small business.** We roast the situation (the notebook, the 47 "price?" DMs, the 11:58 pm caption panic). We never roast the seller.
3. **Show the app doing the work.** Real screens, sample data, clearly labelled.

### Why anyone follows us (the promise, repeat it everywhere)

> "Every week: one dukaan problem, roasted and fixed. Free."

Plus three standing reasons, each one a recurring series (full detail in section 10):

| Series | What the follower gets | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Dukaan Doctor** | One real shop problem, diagnosed and fixed in under 60 seconds | Every Tuesday |
| **0 to 20** | The founder's honest, public attempt to get the first 20 sellers. Wins and fails both posted | Weekly reel plus daily Stories |
| **Free Template Friday** | A free thing to copy: win-back messages in Hindi and English, a festive posting plan, a reorder sheet | Every Friday, comment a keyword to get it |

## 3. Colour system

The Instagram palette is the **ledger palette** from the landing page's own stylesheet: indigo ink on warm paper, like a printed shop ledger, with one marigold accent. It reads as Indian, trustworthy and calm, and nothing like the purple-gradient look of generic AI apps.

> Note for you: the live website currently also carries an iOS-blue theme (`#0062c9`). Brand.md specifies the slate-indigo system. Pick one before launch so your profile and website match. This file assumes the ledger indigo.

### 3.1 Tokens

| Token | Hex | RGB | Role | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper** | `#F7F5EF` | 247, 245, 239 | Main background | 60% of every light post. Never use pure white `#FFFFFF` as a background |
| **Paper 2** | `#EFECE3` | 239, 236, 227 | Secondary surface | Cards on paper, tag chips, receipt paper |
| **Line** | `#DCD8CC` | 220, 216, 204 | Rules and borders | Ledger lines, dividers, card borders at 2 px |
| **Ink** | `#101828` | 16, 24, 40 | Main text | All headlines and body text on light backgrounds. Never use pure black `#000000` |
| **Ink Soft** | `#475467` | 71, 84, 103 | Secondary text | Sub-lines, tags, "Illustrative example" chips |
| **Indigo** | `#1E3A8A` | 30, 58, 138 | Brand colour | Proof slides, the 1T mark, buttons, key words in text |
| **Indigo Deep** | `#16306F` | 22, 48, 111 | Pressed or depth | Shadows of indigo shapes, phone slide backgrounds |
| **Sky** | `#A9C1F5` | 169, 193, 245 | Indigo's light partner | Secondary text on Indigo or Night only |
| **Marigold** | `#F5A623` | 245, 166, 35 | The one accent | Highlighter swash, the margin line, the "one thing to do" arrow, the tap ripple |
| **Night** | `#111827` | 17, 24, 39 | Dark card and dark slides | Number slides, reel end cards, "warning" slides |
| **Money Green** | `#34D399` | 52, 211, 153 | Money kept or gained | Only on Night or Ink backgrounds, only for money the seller keeps |
| **Leak** | `#C4320A` | 196, 50, 10 | Money lost | Only for money leaking (fees, stockouts, lost customers). Maximum one Leak element per slide |

### 3.2 Contrast pairs (checked, WCAG ratio)

| Text colour | on background | Ratio | Allowed for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | Paper | 16.3 | Everything |
| Ink Soft | Paper | 7.1 | Everything |
| Indigo | Paper | 9.5 | Everything |
| Paper | Indigo | 9.5 | Everything |
| Paper | Night | 16.3 | Everything |
| Marigold | Night or Ink | 8.8 | Headlines and numbers |
| Marigold | Indigo | 5.1 | Headlines 48 px and up only |
| Money Green | Night | 9.2 | Numbers only |
| Sky | Indigo | 5.7 | Sub-lines 36 px and up |
| Leak | Paper | 5.1 | Numbers and one short word |
| Ink | Marigold | 8.8 | Text sitting on a marigold sticker |
| **Marigold** | **Paper** | **3.1** | **Never as text.** Only as the highlighter swash behind Ink text, or as a shape |

### 3.3 Colour budget per post
- Light posts: 60% Paper and Paper 2, 25% Ink text and Line, 10% Indigo, 5% Marigold.
- Dark posts: 60% Night, 25% Paper text, 10% Indigo, 5% Marigold or Money Green.
- Marigold appears **once or twice per slide at most**. It points at the one thing that matters. If everything is marigold, nothing is.
- **Photo and AI scenes** are graded toward this palette: warm cream highlights (near Paper), navy-tinted shadows (near Night), wood and skin stay natural, and the only saturated colour in frame is one marigold object or the red cloth of the Khata (see the cast, section 7).

### 3.4 Banned colours and effects
- Purple, violet, lavender, magenta, neon anything, rainbow, holographic.
- Any gradient **between two hues** (for example blue to purple). A single-hue scrim (Night at 0% to 75% opacity) over a photo for text legibility is allowed.
- Teal-and-orange "Hollywood" grading.
- Glows, lens flares, bevels, 3D chrome text, drop shadows on text.

## 4. Typography

All four fonts are free Google Fonts and all are in Canva. They match the app's own stack: a clean grotesque, a monospace for arithmetic, and Noto for Indian scripts.

| Role | Font | Weights | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Headline** | Inter | ExtraBold 800, Bold 700 | Hooks, slide headlines, reel titles |
| **Body** | Inter | Medium 500, SemiBold 600 | Sentences, captions burned into reels |
| **Numbers and labels** | JetBrains Mono | Medium 500, Bold 700 | ₹ amounts, counts, "Day 1", tags, series sticker, arithmetic receipts |
| **Hindi (Devanagari)** | Noto Sans Devanagari | Bold 700, Medium 500 | Any line written in Devanagari. Tamil or Kannada lines use Noto Sans Tamil or Noto Sans Kannada |
| **The notebook voice** | Kalam | Bold 700 | Handwritten annotations only: circles, arrows, "ye dekho", margin notes. Maximum one Kalam element per slide |

### 4.1 Type scale (carousel 1080×1350)

| Element | Font and weight | Size | Line height | Tracking | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook headline (slide 1) | Inter 800 | 104 to 120 px | 1.02 | -3% | Ink on Paper, or Paper on Night or Indigo |
| Slide headline | Inter 800 | 76 to 88 px | 1.05 | -2.5% | Ink |
| Body sentence | Inter 500 | 42 to 46 px | 1.3 | -1% | Ink or Ink Soft |
| Big number | JetBrains Mono 700 | 160 to 240 px | 1.0 | -4% | Ink, Marigold (on dark), Money Green or Leak |
| Arithmetic line | JetBrains Mono 500 | 36 to 40 px | 1.4 | 0 | Ink Soft |
| Label or tag | JetBrains Mono 500, capitals | 24 to 26 px | 1.0 | +8% | Ink Soft on Paper 2 chip |
| Kalam annotation | Kalam 700 | 48 to 60 px | 1.1 | 0 | Indigo (on light) or Marigold (on dark) |

### 4.2 Type scale (reels 1080×1920)
- On-screen hook text: Inter 800, 92 to 110 px, maximum 7 words per line, 2 lines maximum.
- Burned-in captions: Inter 700, 54 px, Ink on a Paper box (padding 18 px by 28 px, corner radius 14 px). The word being spoken turns Indigo. Maximum 2 lines on screen.
- End card line: Inter 800, 80 px.

### 4.3 Writing rules on visuals
- **Sentence case** everywhere. ALL CAPS only for tags and labels (under 26 px).
- **No em dashes.** Use a comma, a full stop or "and". No en dashes for ranges either: write "10 to 15".
- **No emoji on visuals.** Emoji do not render the same on every phone and Brand.md bans them as icons. Use the stroke icon set (section 6.6).
- **Maximum one exclamation mark per post**, and usually zero.
- **Money:** ₹ sign, Indian grouping (₹1,00,000 not ₹100,000), "lakh" and "crore" spelled out in sentences.
- **Hinglish is written in Roman letters** for hooks and jokes. Use Devanagari only when the whole slide is Hindi, or for one short cultural word (शुभ लाभ, दुकान).
- **Standard Hinglish spellings** (always spell them this way): dukaan, kya, nahi, hai, hain, bhai, didi, aap, tu, kaun, kaise, kyun, abhi, sab, kuch, bas, wapas, bechna, bika, khatam, maal, hisaab, khata, jaldi, pakka, yaar, arre.
- **Line breaks carry the joke.** Put the punchline on its own line, and never break a line in the middle of a name, a number or a ₹ amount.

### 4.4 The marigold highlighter (our signature text treatment)
On light slides, the one key phrase of a headline gets a highlighter swash behind it:
- Shape: rectangle, the full width of the phrase plus 12 px each side, 58% of the cap height, sitting on the lower half of the letters.
- Colour: Marigold `#F5A623` at 70% opacity. The edges are slightly irregular, like a real highlighter pen, with -1.5° rotation.
- Maximum **one highlighted phrase per slide**. Text stays Ink.

## 5. Layout and grid

### 5.1 Canvas sizes
| Format | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Carousel slide | 1080×1350 (4:5) | All slides in a carousel are the same size |
| Single image post | 1080×1350 (4:5) | |
| Reel | 1080×1920 (9:16) | Shoot and generate at 9:16, never crop a 16:9 |
| Reel cover | 1080×1920, designed to survive the 1080×1440 (3:4) profile-grid crop | Key text stays inside the centre 3:4 area |
| Story | 1080×1920 | |

### 5.2 Carousel margins and safe area
- Outer margin: **88 px** left and right, **120 px** top, **140 px** bottom.
- The profile grid shows 4:5 posts cropped to 3:4, which trims about 34 px off each side. Nothing important lives within 60 px of the side edges.
- Content column: 904 px wide.
- **Ledger margin line:** one vertical Marigold line, 4 px wide, at x = 64 px, running from y = 0 to y = 1350. It is on every light text slide. This is the "ruled ledger" signature.
- **Ledger rules (optional texture):** horizontal Line `#DCD8CC` rules, 1.5 px, every 56 px, at 35% opacity, behind text slides. Text sits on the rules like writing in a register.
- **Slide counter:** JetBrains Mono 500, 24 px, Ink Soft, top right at (992, 72), right aligned, format "2/6".
- **Series sticker:** top left at (88, 64). See section 6.3.

### 5.3 Reel safe zones (1080×1920)
- **Top 220 px:** keep clear (the app's header and the progress bar sit here).
- **Bottom 420 px:** keep clear (caption, username, audio and buttons sit here).
- **Right 150 px:** keep clear from y = 900 to y = 1700 (like, comment, share, save icons).
- **Left 60 px:** keep clear.
- **Hook text zone:** y = 300 to y = 760, centred horizontally, inside x = 80 to x = 930.
- **Caption zone:** y = 1180 to y = 1420.

## 6. Signature elements

These are what make a stranger recognise a One Tap Manager post in half a second without seeing the name.

### 6.1 The 1T mark
- "1T" in JetBrains Mono Bold, Paper on an Indigo rounded square (corner radius 22% of the square's width).
- On posts: 64×64 px, bottom right at (928, 1198) on carousels, and centred on reel end cards at 200×200 px.

### 6.2 Tag chips (honesty labels)
Rounded rectangle (corner radius 10 px, not a pill), Paper 2 fill, 2 px Line border, JetBrains Mono 500 capitals at 22 px, Ink Soft text, padding 10 px by 16 px.
- `ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE`: on any slide or scene with a number that is not a real seller's real number.
- `SAMPLE SHOP DATA`: on any real app screen recording made with the app's sample data.
- `AI GENERATED`: on every slide or reel that contains an AI-made image or clip. **Mandatory** (Brand.md section 8 and Indian IT Rules). Place it bottom left at (88, 1230) on carousels and at (60, 1460) on reels, so it stays above Instagram's caption area.
- Also switch on Instagram's own "AI info" label when uploading any post that contains AI visuals.

### 6.3 Series sticker
- Rounded rectangle, corner radius 12 px, Indigo fill, JetBrains Mono 700, 24 px, Paper text, capitals, +8% tracking, padding 12 px by 18 px.
- Text format: `DUKAAN DOCTOR · EP 03`, `0 TO 20 · DAY 1`, `FREE TEMPLATE FRIDAY`.
- A 10 px Marigold dot sits before the text, like a recording light.

### 6.4 The arithmetic receipt
Whenever we show money, we show the maths on a receipt:
- Receipt shape: Paper 2, 640 px wide, a zig-zag torn edge at the bottom (12 px teeth), 2 px Line border on the sides.
- Lines in JetBrains Mono 500, 36 px, Ink Soft, amounts right aligned in Ink.
- The total line sits above a dashed 2 px Ink rule. The total is JetBrains Mono 700, 44 px, in Leak (money lost) or Indigo (our price).
- Example (from our real pricing page):
  ```
  Analytics & LTV app           ₹1,600
  Inventory & reorder app       ₹2,500
  Win-back / retention app      ₹1,500
  Reviews & reputation app      ₹1,200
  Order management app            ₹800
  ---------------------------------------
  Typical monthly total         ₹7,600
  One Tap Manager Max             ₹999
  ```
  Label: `TYPICAL SHOPIFY APP COSTS · BEFORE % FEES`.

### 6.5 The tap ripple (our motion logo)
- A Marigold dot (24 px) where a finger "taps", followed by one ring that expands from 24 px to 180 px over 420 ms, fading from 100% to 0% opacity, ease-out.
- It appears on every "one tap" moment in reels, and it is the logo sting on every end card (section 9.6).

### 6.6 Icons
- The app's stroke icon style: 2 px stroke at 48 px size, rounded caps and joins, no fills, Ink on light and Paper on dark.
- Use icons for: shop awning, rupee, box (stock), person (customer), calendar (posts), chat bubble (WhatsApp message), receipt, star (reviews), truck (delivery).
- **Never** use emoji as icons on visuals. Never use the real WhatsApp, Instagram, Shopify or Amazon logos on our designs. Write the name in plain text instead.

### 6.7 Phone frame
- For real app screenshots: a plain phone frame, Night `#111827` bezel 18 px, corner radius 64 px, no brand, no notch drawing, no reflections.
- The screen content is always a **real screenshot or screen recording** of One Tap Manager. AI never draws the app's screens (see section 8.5).

## 7. The world bible: faceless cast, set and props

We never show a face. Instead we have a recurring cast of hands and objects that followers will learn to recognise. **Use these exact descriptions in every AI prompt.** Consistency is what makes a faceless page feel like a brand.

### 7.1 The cast

**THE SELLER'S HANDS** (our main character, always faceless)
- Medium-brown Indian skin, adult hands, natural and slightly imperfect (visible knuckle creases, a faint henna fade on one fingertip is allowed in festive posts).
- Short, clean, natural nails with a nude-beige polish.
- **Right wrist:** one thin plain gold bangle.
- **Left wrist:** a red-and-yellow cotton kalava thread, slightly frayed.
- **Sleeves:** a mustard-marigold cotton kurta (close to `#E0A030`), three-quarter sleeves with a thin cream piping at the cuff.
- Shot from POV (first person, looking down) or from over-the-shoulder behind the head, which is always cropped out of frame above the collarbone.
- Real reference: photograph your own (or a helper's) hands wearing exactly this and attach the photos to every hands prompt.

**THE KHATA** (the comedy side-character: the old red ledger that is "about to retire")
- A classic Indian bahi-khata: a long, narrow ledger bound in **deep red cloth** (close to `#A11D21`), with a **white-and-yellow cotton string** tied around it, with worn, frayed corners.
- Inside: cream, slightly yellowed pages with faint blue ruled lines, and handwritten entries in **blue ballpoint** mixing Hindi and English ("Kurti M - 3", "Priya didi 1200 udhaar", crossings out, sums circled).
- The first page has "शुभ लाभ" handwritten at the top in red ink with a small swastika-free decorative border. Keep the religious symbols out, "शुभ लाभ" alone is enough.
- Details: a faint circular chai-stain ring on the cover, one sticky note poking out of the pages.
- Personality: loyal, overworked, slightly dramatic. It is "Version 1.0, since 2019". It never gets mocked cruelly; it gets a retirement party.

**THE PHONE**
- A mid-range Android phone with a dark grey back, inside a **transparent silicone case that has yellowed slightly**, with a **folded ₹10 note tucked behind the phone inside the case** (a very Indian detail).
- A **tiny crack in the top-left corner of the screen protector**, not the screen.
- When the screen shows content, it is **solid chroma green `#00B140`** in the AI image, so you can drop a real screenshot in later (see section 8.5). When the screen is off, it is black with a soft reflection of the window.

**THE CHAI**
- A small ribbed "cutting chai" glass, filled two-thirds, light milky-brown chai with a thin skin on top, standing on the desk. Sometimes a steel saucer under it.

**THE STOCK**
- Clothing: folded cotton kurtis in dusty rose, sage green, indigo block-print and ivory, stacked in a neat pile of 5 to 20. Each has a small white paper size tag (S, M, L, XL) on a thread.
- Jewellery: oxidised-silver jhumkas and a kundan-style necklace on a small velvet tray in deep bottle-green.
- Perfume: two or three small attar bottles with cut-glass bodies and gold caps, amber and pale gold liquids.
- Packaging: kraft-paper mailer bags, a roll of brown packing tape, a stack of printed shipping labels (label text blurred).

### 7.2 The set: "the Dukaan desk"
- A warm wooden desk (sheesham or teak tone), wood grain visible, a few scratches, against a **cream lime-plastered wall** (near Paper `#F7F5EF`).
- On the left, a window with a **simple iron window grill** in a rectangular pattern. Soft morning daylight streams through it and casts faint grill-pattern shadows on the desk.
- Behind, slightly out of focus: an open steel almirah or a wooden rack with folded stock, and a plastic ring-light stand leaning in the corner (the seller shoots reels here too).
- Props that may appear (never more than five at once): the Khata, the phone, the chai, a basic Casio-style calculator, a ballpoint pen, a stapler, a few crumpled receipts, yellow sticky notes, a small potted money plant.
- **Festive variant** (Diwali and wedding season): one lit brass diya on the desk corner, a small string of warm fairy lights out of focus, marigold flower petals on the desk.

### 7.3 Camera language
| Shot | Lens (35 mm equivalent) | Aperture | Height and angle | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| POV | 24 mm | f/2.8 | Eye height, looking down at 60° | Hands with phone, hands with stock |
| Top-down flat lay | 35 mm | f/5.6 | Directly overhead, 90° | Desk chaos, before and after |
| Hero prop | 85 mm macro | f/2.0 | Desk height, 10° above | The Khata, the chai, a single kurti |
| Wide set | 28 mm | f/4 | Standing height, 30° down | The Dukaan desk and room |

- Shallow depth of field on hero shots. The background is always softly blurred so text can sit on it.
- For video, the camera moves slowly: 5% push-ins, gentle handheld sway on POV shots. No drone moves, no fast spins, and no Dutch angles except on the comic "fail" beat.

### 7.4 Light and grade
- **Key light:** soft natural window light from camera left, 5,200 to 5,600 K, as in a real Indian home at 9 to 10 am.
- **Fill:** bounced from the cream wall, low contrast, shadows open but present.
- **Night scenes** (the 11:58 pm post): a single warm bedside lamp at 2,700 K plus the cool glow of the phone screen, shadows in Night `#111827` tones.
- **Grade:** highlights toward cream Paper `#F7F5EF`, shadows toward navy Night `#111827`, skin natural and warm, wood warm brown, and saturation at 90%. Only the Khata's red, the kurta's mustard and marigold accents are allowed to pop.
- **Texture:** real photographic grain at a fine level (like ISO 400), no plastic smoothness, no HDR halos.

### 7.5 Master style block (paste at the end of every AI image prompt)

```
Style: photorealistic editorial lifestyle photography of a real small Indian home-run
shop, shot on a full-frame camera, natural soft window light from the left at about
5,400 K, gentle bounce fill from a cream lime-plaster wall, fine natural film grain,
true-to-life textures (wood grain, cotton weave, paper fibres, skin creases), calm
and warm mood. Colour grade: cream highlights close to #F7F5EF, navy-tinted shadows
close to #111827, natural warm skin and wood, saturation about 90 percent, with the
only strong colours being deep red cloth (#A11D21), mustard-marigold (#E0A030 to
#F5A623) and deep indigo (#1E3A8A) where described. Composition leaves the
described empty area clean and uncluttered for text to be added later.
No human faces anywhere in frame, no heads, only hands and forearms if people appear.
```

### 7.6 Master negative prompt (paste into every negative prompt field, or at the end as "Avoid:")

```
Avoid: any human face, head, eyes, lips or hair; extra fingers, fused fingers,
missing fingers, deformed hands, long acrylic nails, plastic-looking skin;
any written text, letters, words, numbers, captions, watermarks, logos, brand
names, app icons, user-interface elements drawn on screens (screens must be
solid chroma green #00B140 or switched off); purple, violet, lavender, magenta,
neon, rainbow or holographic colours; colour gradients; teal-and-orange grading;
lens flares, glow, bloom, HDR halos; stock-photo smiles; Western coffee mugs,
lattes, MacBooks, dollar signs, Western shop interiors; clutter beyond the props
described; blurry or smeared details; cartoon or 3D-render look unless the prompt
asks for it.
```

## 8. AI production rules

### 8.1 Which tool for what (general guidance, check the latest versions)
| Job | Use |
|---|---|
| Photoreal scenes with your reference hands and props | An image model that accepts reference images and keeps characters consistent (for example Google Gemini image editing, ChatGPT image generation, or Midjourney with character and style references) |
| Slides with exact on-screen text | Design the slide in Canva using this file's fonts. If you must generate text inside an image, use a model known for accurate text (for example Ideogram or ChatGPT image generation), then check every letter |
| Short cinematic clips (3 to 8 seconds) | Image-to-video: generate the first frame as an image, then animate it (for example Google Veo, Kling or Runway). Always start from an approved first frame so the look stays on brand |
| Voiceover | Your own voice (best for trust), or one fixed AI voice used on every reel (section 9.5) |

### 8.2 The order of work for every AI visual
1. Attach the reference images the post lists (hands, Khata, props, real screenshots).
2. Paste the **instruction block** from section 0.
3. Paste the post's **scene prompt**, which already includes the master style block and the negative prompt.
4. Generate 4 options. Pick one on four checks: hands correct (5 fingers, bangle right wrist, kalava left wrist), no face, no stray text, palette on brand.
5. For video, animate the approved still. Never generate video from text alone for hero shots.
6. Add all text, tags and stickers yourself in Canva or CapCut using this file's specs. AI draws the scene; you set the words.
7. Add the `AI GENERATED` chip.

### 8.3 Keeping the cast consistent across 12 posts
- Build a **reference pack** once: 6 photos of the hands (holding phone, typing, holding chai, holding a kurti, pointing, flat on desk), 4 photos of the Khata (closed, open, cover close-up, string), 2 of the phone (front with green screen, back with the ₹10 note), 1 of the desk set.
- Attach the same pack to every generation. If a tool offers a "seed" or "character reference" setting, reuse the same one across all posts.
- If you cannot get a real red Khata, generate one master image of it first (prompt in the 12-posts file, Asset A0), approve it, and attach that image in every later prompt.

### 8.4 What AI must never make
- The One Tap Manager app interface, dashboards, or any screen of the app. Only real screenshots.
- A seller's face, a customer's face, or a real person.
- A review, testimonial, WhatsApp chat, or DM that looks real. Fake DMs for jokes must be obviously styled as our own graphic (section 9.4), with generic names ("Customer 1") or no names.
- Real brand logos (Instagram, WhatsApp, Shopify, Amazon, Meesho, banks, UPI apps).
- Currency notes in close-up detail (keep any ₹ note small, folded and partly hidden).

### 8.5 Green-screen phone method
Every AI image or clip that shows a phone screen uses solid `#00B140` chroma green on the screen. In CapCut or Canva:
1. Remove the green (Chroma key, intensity about 30, shadow 0).
2. Place the real screenshot or screen recording under it.
3. Add a 6% white reflection layer at 10% opacity over the screen for realism.
4. Add the `SAMPLE SHOP DATA` chip near the phone.

## 9. Motion and sound language (reels)

### 9.1 Rhythm
- **The first frame is the hook.** Text is on screen from frame 1. No logo intro, no "hi guys", no fade in from black (except the Warning Screen format).
- **Target lengths:** 15 to 30 s for most reels, and 45 s maximum. The loop point: the last frame should cut cleanly back into the first so replays feel seamless.
- **Shot length:** 1.2 to 2.5 s on average. Cut on the beat or on the spoken punchline.
- **The "rule of 3 seconds":** by second 3 the viewer must know who this is for (a small seller) and what the tension is.

### 9.2 Text animation
- Headline in: scale from 92% to 100% with a 4% overshoot, 280 ms, ease-out (cubic-bezier 0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1), plus opacity 0 to 100% in 180 ms.
- Headline out: opacity 100% to 0% in 160 ms, no movement.
- Word-by-word captions: each word appears on its spoken syllable. The current word is Indigo and the rest are Ink.
- Numbers counting: JetBrains Mono, rolling digits (like a counter) over 600 to 900 ms, ease-out.
- Marigold highlighter: draws from left to right over 350 ms, like a pen stroke.

### 9.3 Transitions (only these three)
1. **Hard cut** on the beat (default).
2. **Page flip:** a ledger page turns from right to left across the frame, 450 ms, used to go from the "old way" to the "new way". This is our signature transition.
3. **Tap ripple wipe:** the marigold ripple from section 6.5 expands to fill the screen with Paper, 500 ms, used to jump into a real app screen recording.

No spins, glitch, zoom-blur, star wipes or 3D cube transitions.

### 9.4 Our "fake chat" graphic style (for jokes)
When a joke needs DMs or messages, never mock up real WhatsApp or Instagram UI. Use our own chat style:
- Bubbles in Paper 2, 2 px Line border, corner radius 28 px, and one sharp corner at the sender's side.
- Text in Inter 600, 40 px, Ink. The sender label is "Customer 1", "Customer 2" and so on, in JetBrains Mono 22 px, Ink Soft.
- The background is Night or Paper, never a copy of a real app background.

### 9.5 Voice
- **First choice: your own voice.** A real founder's voice builds trust for a zero-customer brand. The face is never needed.
- If AI: pick **one** warm Indian-English voice with light natural Hinglish (a male or female voice in their late 20s to early 30s), and use the same voice on every reel. Settings: medium pace (160 to 170 words per minute), slight smile in the voice, dry, deadpan delivery on punchlines (no laugh track, no "hahaha").
- **Delivery style:** conversational and low-key sarcastic, like a friend telling you something true over chai. Pause for half a beat before every punchline. Never shout. Never sound like an ad.
- **Recording:** a quiet room, phone 20 cm from the mouth, no music under the raw file. Normalise to -14 LUFS for Instagram.

### 9.6 Sound
- **Sonic logo** (every reel ends with it): a single soft finger-tap on glass, then a warm two-note marimba chime (a rising fifth, about 700 ms). Visual: the tap ripple on the 1T mark.
- **Music:** only from Instagram's own audio library at the time of posting (licensing is covered that way). Choose a trending track that fits the mood the post specifies. Keep music at -18 to -22 dB under the voice.
- **SFX kit** (stay consistent): paper rustle and page flip (the Khata), phone buzz (DMs), soft "bonk" (the fail beat), cash-drawer "ting" (money kept), a low heartbeat (tension), record scratch (reality break), steel glass clink (chai).

### 9.7 End card (last 1.5 s of every reel)
- Background: Paper.
- The 1T mark 200×200 px centred at y = 700, with the tap ripple animating once.
- Line 1: the series promise, Inter 800, 64 px, Ink, centred at y = 980: "One dukaan problem. Fixed every week."
- Line 2: JetBrains Mono 500, 34 px, Ink Soft, y = 1080: "@onetapmanager"
- Sonic logo plays.

## 10. Voice and humour on Instagram

### 10.1 The dial
Brand.md's five attributes stay: Plain, Honest, Useful, Warm, India-first. On Instagram, we add one dial on top: **Funny.**

| Attribute | On the app | On Instagram |
|---|---|---|
| Plain | High | High |
| Honest | High | High (never negotiable) |
| Useful | Highest | High (every post teaches or gives something) |
| Warm | Medium | High |
| India-first | High | Highest |
| **Funny** | Off | **High but dry**: observational, deadpan, self-aware, Hinglish |

### 10.2 The humour style
It's observational, deadpan, self-roasting Indian comedy: the kind that points at an everyday truth everyone recognises, says it with a straight face, and then lets the silence do the work. The model is the friend at the chai tapri who says the true thing everyone was thinking.

**The four humour rules:**
1. **Punch at the situation, never at the seller.** The notebook, the 47 "price?" DMs, the cousin who "makes websites", the phone falling on your face: those are the villains. The seller is always the hero who is tired, smart and doing too much.
2. **Every joke must be true.** If a seller would not nod and say "haan yaar, yahi hota hai", cut it.
3. **Roast, then fix.** The joke earns attention and the fix earns the follow. There's no post that is only a joke, and none that is only a lecture.
4. **Roast ourselves first.** "We have zero customers" and "Our founder also wrote captions at midnight" make the roasts of everything else feel fair.

**Joke bank (true, relatable, safe to reuse):**
- The DMs: "Price?", "PP", "M available?", "COD hai?", "Last price kya hai?", "Interested" (and then silence).
- Family: "Beta, online business se kuch hota bhi hai?", "Sharma ji ke bete ka bhi business hai, uska toh website hai".
- The cousin: "Mera cousin website bana dega, next week pakka."
- The Khata: "Accounting software: red notebook, version 1.0, since 2019."
- Time: "Caption likhne baitha 11:58 pm, post hua 2:13 am."
- Reach: "10k followers, 3 orders. Likes se kiraya nahi bharta."
- Returns: "Customer: 'size chhota hai'. Also customer: ordered S instead of L."
- Stock: "Bestseller khatam, tab pata chala ki bestseller tha."
- Apps: "Five apps, five bills, zero talk to each other. Like a family WhatsApp group."

**Banned humour:**
- Jokes about customers being stupid, poor or "cheap". They are our sellers' livelihood.
- Gender jokes ("aunties", "ladies and their shopping"), regional, caste, religious, body or accent jokes.
- Real people: no real creators', celebrities' or politicians' names, faces, voices or catchphrases. We aim for their comedic sharpness, never their identity.
- Competitor mockery by name. We compare facts (app categories and prices) and never insult anyone.
- Dark or crude jokes, swearing, and innuendo.

### 10.3 Language mix
- The default is **Hinglish in Roman script**, about 60% English words and 40% Hindi, and the kind of mix a Tier 1 or Tier 2 seller uses in DMs.
- Hooks: short, and often Hindi-led ("Bestseller khatam, aur tujhe pata bhi nahi").
- Teaching lines: clearer, more English ("Check these 3 numbers every Monday").
- Every reel has burned-in captions in the same language as the voice.
- Once a month, make a fully Hindi version of the best reel (Devanagari captions). Once the audience asks, try Tamil and Kannada versions (the app supports both).

### 10.4 Captions (the text under the post)
Structure, every time:
1. **Line 1 (the only line people see before "more"):** a second hook, not a repeat of the visual. Maximum 12 words.
2. **Lines 2 to 5:** the value in plain sentences. One idea per line. Blank line between ideas.
3. **The honest line:** "Numbers are an illustrative example." or "Real app, sample shop data." (whichever applies).
4. **The CTA:** one ask only. Comment a keyword, save, send to a friend, or follow for the series.
5. **Keyword line for Instagram search** (plain words, not hashtags): for example "clothing business tips, small business India, online boutique".
6. **Hashtags:** 3 to 5 maximum, at the very end.

Caption rules: no em dashes, a maximum of one emoji (optional, never as a bullet), a maximum of one exclamation mark, and no "link in bio" without saying what is there.

**Approved CTAs:**
- "Comment STORE and I'll send you the link to make your free website."
- "Save this for Monday morning."
- "Send this to that one friend who's still using a notebook."
- "Follow for one dukaan problem, fixed every week."
- "Comment WINBACK for the free message templates, in Hindi and English."

### 10.5 Hashtag sets (rotate, 3 to 5 per post)
- **Core:** #smallbusinessindia #instagramseller #onlinebusinessindia
- **Clothing:** #kurtibusiness #boutiqueowner #clothingbusiness
- **Jewellery:** #jewellerybusiness #artificialjewellery #jewellerysellers
- **Perfume:** #attar #perfumebusiness
- **Founder series:** #buildinpublic #startupindia

### 10.6 Alt text (Instagram SEO, fill in "Advanced settings" on every post)
Describe the image plainly and include one search phrase. For example: "Red Indian ledger notebook on a wooden shop desk next to a phone, illustrating shop management for small clothing sellers."

### 10.7 Comment replies (the voice keeps going in replies)
- Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes, and within 24 hours after that.
- Same humour, plain help. For example:
  - "Price?" (as a joke comment) → "Free. Aur haan, ye wala 'price?' reply karne mein humein bhi maza aaya."
  - "Is this really free?" → "Free plan is ₹0, forever. Max is ₹999 a month if you want supplier orders. We never take a cut of your sales. Full list on onetapmanager.com."
  - "Hindi mein hai?" → "Haan, the app works in Hindi, and in Tamil and Kannada too."
- Never argue. Never promise results. Move anything account-related to DMs.

### 10.8 Comment-keyword automation
Promising "comment X and I'll DM you" only works if every commenter gets the DM. Use a Meta-approved comment-to-DM tool (for example ManyChat) or reply by hand within a few hours. Never promise it on a post if nobody can send the DMs that week.

## 11. Honesty and legal rules (non-negotiable)

1. **AI GENERATED** chip on every AI visual, plus Instagram's AI label switched on.
2. **ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE** chip on every number that isn't a real seller's real number. Show the arithmetic where money is involved.
3. **SAMPLE SHOP DATA** chip on every screen recording made with sample data.
4. No fake customers, reviews, testimonials, "10,000 sellers" counters or results. We say "we are new" proudly.
5. Only claim features that are live in `Product.md`. Current limits to respect:
   - **WhatsApp:** the app prepares tap-to-send WhatsApp links. It does not auto-send. Say "tap to send", never "we send it for you".
   - **Ad analytics** (Meta and Google Ads): demo figures only for now, so don't market them.
   - **Supplier orders and reorder maths** are Max features. While launch mode is on, say "Free during launch".
   - **Languages:** the app works in English, Hindi, Tamil and Kannada. Its AI writing can produce other Indian languages, but say "Hindi, Tamil and Kannada" when describing the app.
   - **Review analysis** needs the seller's reviews file.
6. Never show real customer names, phone numbers, addresses or real chats. Blur or use "Customer 1".
7. Use only Instagram-library music, and never a copyrighted film clip, meme template footage or a celebrity's voice.
8. Never copy Instagram's own warning or sensitive-content screen pixel for pixel. Our "Warning" format uses our own design (template T6 in the 12-posts file).

# Campaign type dropdown: seven campaigns, each with its own audience and voices

## Request (from the user)

"In 'Why are you doing it' make it a drop down and add more campaigns also in
that, and configure the backend message writer according to that."

## Campaign types

| id | Label | Audience | Product line | Suggestion | Default offer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| winback | Win back quiet customers | At Risk + Hibernating within a year | favourite | Apriori | flat |
| festival | Festival offer | Champions, Loyal, top 20% spend, At Risk | favourite | Apriori | percent |
| second_order | Second-order nudge | one order only, 14 to 60 days ago | their first purchase | Apriori | flat |
| cross_sell | Goes well with what you bought | own X of a strong rule (lift >= 1.2), not Y | X | Y (required) | percent |
| restock | Time to restock | past their usual reorder gap for a product (median gap 7 to 120 days) | the product due | none | none |
| vip | VIP early access | Champions | favourite | Apriori | none; "what is new" note required |
| thank_you | Thank you + review request | last order 2 to 14 days ago | what they just bought | none | none |

## Decisions

- Offer can be **No discount** for any type. Such campaigns still get a silent
  tracking code: the link carries `ref=` instead of `code=`, the storefront
  applies it quietly (0 off, no "code applied" box), and the order is still
  attributed in the tracker.
- 10 voices per type (70 total), part-based as before, plus a no-discount
  offer line per voice.
- Cooldowns per type: winback 45, festival 14, second_order 30, cross_sell 21,
  restock 14, vip 14, thank_you 30 days.
- WhatsApp API: a second template "otm_campaign_update_v1" (no offer) is
  submitted alongside the offer template; no-discount campaigns use it.
- The ideas cards become "start this campaign" shortcuts; the dropdown shows
  how many customers each type would reach now.
- The automatic every-second-Monday campaign stays festival / win-back.

# Marketing Campaign round 2: varied messages, editable contacts, tracker, WhatsApp Embedded Signup

Builds on round 1 (`df3b019` "Marketing Big", already live): discount codes,
campaign engine, writer, picture, WhatsApp module, fortnightly scheduler.

## Requests (from the user)

1. Confirm & save on the mapping screen gives no sign it is working; a seller
   clicks it several times.
2. Give them the link, and a proper campaign analyzer / tracker, built the way
   the best campaign managers measure; then suggest other campaign types.
3. Connecting WhatsApp (three IDs + token) is too technical. Do it like
   Instagram: testers added by hand until Meta access is granted.
4. No place to add phone numbers later; it does not show what each customer
   will get.
5. Not the same message for everyone: backend logic, apriori for what they
   like, 10 custom messages, filled with customer name and products, so it
   feels premium.

## Decisions

- WhatsApp: **Embedded Signup** (Meta's own popup: log in with Facebook,
  verify the number by SMS code). Sellers are added as testers on the Meta app
  until the app is approved as a Tech Provider, exactly like Instagram. Option
  to keep using the WhatsApp Business app on the same number (coexistence:
  no /register, two smb_app_data syncs). Pasting IDs stays as "Advanced".
  Meta bills message fees to the seller's own WhatsApp account.
- Messages: no AI per campaign. 10 hand-written "voices" per reason
  (win-back, festival), each built from parts (opener, what-you-bought line,
  recommendation line, offer line, sign-off) so a missing product or
  recommendation never leaves a broken sentence. Voices are dealt round-robin
  in a per-campaign shuffled order, so neighbours never get the same one.
  Email subjects rotate the same way.
- Recommendation: apriori association rules over orders (support, confidence,
  lift). For each customer, the highest-lift consequent of anything they
  bought that they have not bought themselves; fallback the best seller in
  their favourite category they have not bought; else none.
- Tracking without new infrastructure: every message link is the shop with
  `?code=` (already) plus `c=<campaign>`; the storefront reports the visit to
  `/api/shop/{handle}/visit`, which stamps the code (first click, clicks). Cart
  pricing with the code stamps "applied". Redemption already stamps the order.
  Funnel: reached → clicked → applied → ordered, plus revenue, discount spent,
  revenue per unit of discount, and "came back any way" from the sales data
  (winback_proof window) against a **holdout**: 10% of the audience (when it
  is 20+) is kept out and not messaged, so lift is measured, not assumed.
- Contacts: phone / email can be added or corrected per customer in the
  draft; saved to a per-seller contact book so the next campaign has them.
- Each customer's exact message is visible (expand the row) and can be edited
  for that one customer; the edit must keep their code.

## Tasks

1. Mapping Confirm & save: busy state, double-click guard, controls disabled
   while saving (smart.js).
2. `analytics.association_rules` + `recommend_for` (apriori, vectorised over a
   basket matrix of the top items, capped for 512MB).
3. `campaign_writer`: 10 voices x 2 reasons, part-based assembly, deal order,
   subjects; seller note line; drop the per-campaign AI call from build.
4. `contacts.py` contact book; engine merges it; `update_draft` accepts
   per-row `contacts` and `message` overrides (code must survive).
5. Holdout in `campaign_engine.build`; holdout recorded on the draft and in
   the send log.
6. Tracking: `discounts.track(seller, code, event)`; `/api/shop/{h}/visit`;
   cart stamps applied; store.js reports the visit once per session.
7. Analyzer: `campaign_engine.analyze(email, campaign_id)` +
   `/api/campaign/{id}/analysis`; UI: KPI tiles, funnel, per-customer status,
   lift vs holdout; copy-link buttons.
8. Other campaign types: data-aware suggestion cards (first-time buyers,
   cross-sell from rules, replenishment, VIP early access, review request).
9. WhatsApp Embedded Signup: `whatsapp.embedded_config`,
   `whatsapp.complete_embedded`; `/api/whatsapp/embedded`; Account pane with
   one "Connect WhatsApp" button, coexistence checkbox, tester note;
   manual form under Advanced.
10. UI: per-row expand (exact message, edit, phone/email), no-contact rows
    first with "Add phone".
11. Tests: extend scripts/test_marketing_campaign.py; keep winback_auto,
    gate1, publisher, onboarding, website_builder (pre-existing fail),
    marketing_module green.
12. Browser check; SUMMARY.md; STATE.md row.

## Admin setup the user must do once (WhatsApp)

Env: `WHATSAPP_APP_ID`, `WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET` (the Meta app's Facebook App
ID/secret, Settings → Basic), `WHATSAPP_CONFIG_ID` (Facebook Login for
Business → Configurations → "WhatsApp Embedded Signup" template). Add the app
domain to the allowed domains; add each seller as a tester until Tech
Provider approval.

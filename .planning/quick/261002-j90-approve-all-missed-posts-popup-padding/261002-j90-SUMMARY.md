# Summary: Approve all that sticks, a "missed posts" line, pop-up padding

## What changed

- `c77038a` fix(state): `user_store.update_state` merges against the stored
  state when another write happened since the request's memoised copy (per
  account version counter; no extra read otherwise). Three-way merge per key:
  dict per sub-key, `[{"id"}]` list per record, else last writer.
- `240a6e4` feat(social): `social.is_missed` / `_missed_notice`; missed drafts
  (before today, or past `publisher.GRACE_HOURS` today) become one
  dismissable "We missed N posts" line (last 7 days). Client `isDecision()`
  keeps it out of counts and Approve all. Week approve skips missed posts.
  Home ETag includes the hour and the dismissal. `/api/managers` ignores it.
- `9fa174c` style(popups): one spacing block at the end of ios.css.

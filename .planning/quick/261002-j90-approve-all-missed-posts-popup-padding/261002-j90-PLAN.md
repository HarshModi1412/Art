# Approve all that sticks, a "missed posts" line, pop-up padding

## Requests (from the user)

1. Approval panel: only offer posts from the day the seller opens the app.
   Plan made on the 1st, opened on the 4th -> one line "We missed 3 posts",
   then only posts from the 4th onward.
2. Approve all: press it, every card leaves, then all but one come back; the
   seller has to press it once per post.
3. Column-mapping dialog (and every pop-up) looks like it has no padding.

## Root cause of 2 (evidence)

- Client is guarded (in-flight cards filtered; one refresh after the queue
  drains), so the cards that come back are really still drafts on the server.
- `user_store.update_state` saves the account's WHOLE state from the copy the
  request memoised at its first read (`request_scope`). The browser runs two
  approvals at once and each spends seconds drawing a picture; the last to
  finish writes every other post back to draft.
- Reproduced: scripts/test_concurrent_approve.py, 2 concurrent approvals ->
  `{'p0': 'draft', 'p1': 'scheduled'}`; 6 over two lanes -> 1 left draft;
  two different keys written at once -> one lost.

## Tasks

1. `update_state`: read the stored state fresh under the lock; merge each
   patched key three-way against what the request last saw (dict per sub-key,
   `[{"id": ...}]` list per record, otherwise last writer). Unchanged keys keep
   whatever another request wrote.
2. `social.pending_insight_cards`: drafts whose day has gone (`is_missed`:
   before today, or past `publisher.GRACE_HOURS` today) are not cards; one
   `notice` card "We missed N posts" (last 7 days) leads the desk; dismiss is
   stored per notice id. Week approve (server) skips missed posts. ETag
   fingerprint includes the hour and the dismissal.
3. Client: render the notice as a dismissable line; `isDecision()` keeps it
   (and the week header) out of every count and out of Approve all.
4. CSS: one pop-up spacing block at the end of ios.css (modal, drawer, cgpt
   card, busy box, journey sheet on phones); table first/last column padding
   on wide screens only.
